ABOUT OUR VILLAGE

Welcome to Raorukula Village

Raorukula is a peaceful village located in Siddipet Rural Mandal, Siddipet District, Telangana. Agriculture is the primary occupation of the villagers, and the village is well connected to Siddipet town.

The village provides essential facilities including a Gram Panchayat, Health Sub-Centre, Post Office, Bus Facility, Government School, and access to various digital government services.
