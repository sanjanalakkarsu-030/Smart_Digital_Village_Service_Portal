💚 Health Tips & Awareness

Follow these simple tips to maintain good health.

💧

Drink Enough Water

Drink enough clean water throughout the day to stay hydrated.

🥗

Eat a Balanced Diet

Eat fruits, vegetables, grains and other nutritious foods.

🏃

Stay Physically Active

Regular walking and physical activity help maintain good health.

🧼

Maintain Hygiene

Wash your hands regularly and keep yourself and your surroundings clean.

😴

Get Enough Sleep

Follow a regular sleep schedule and get enough rest every day.

🩺

Regular Health Check-ups

Regular check-ups can help identify health problems early.

🦟

Prevent Mosquito Diseases

Keep surroundings clean and avoid stagnant water near your home.

🚭

Avoid Tobacco

Avoid tobacco products and other harmful substances.
