Government Scheme Details

PM Kisan Samman Nidhi

Description

Provides financial assistance of ₹6,000 per year to eligible farmer families.

Benefits

  • ₹6,000 financial assistance every year
  • Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT)
  • Supports small and marginal farmers

Eligibility

  • Indian Farmer
  • Own agricultural land

Required Documents

  • Aadhaar Card
  • Bank Passbook
  • Land Records

PM Awas Yojana

Description

Benefits

  • Affordable Housing

  • Permanent House

Eligibility

  • Economically Weaker Sections
  • No Permanent House

Required Documents

  • Aadhaar Card
  • Income Certificate
  • Residence Proof

Ayushman Bharat (PM-JAY)

Description

Provides free health insurance coverage up to ₹5 lakh per family per year.

Benefits

  • Cashless treatment
  • Free hospitalization
  • Coverage up to ₹5 lakh

Eligibility

  • Eligible families under SECC database

Required Documents

  • Aadhaar Card
  • Ration Card
